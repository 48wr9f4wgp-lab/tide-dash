## What changes on screen

The primary scan order is tide direction, next high/low time, tide graph, wind/wave/rain values. Numbers are larger and units are visually secondary. Large widgets use three aligned value cards; medium widgets use one compact value row instead of squeezing three multi-line cards. The tide label is plain Japanese, such as '潮が上がる', with its hourly prediction window retained. A turning interval still says '干潮の時間帯', not a claimed instantaneous flow reversal. Station name/reference distance and display time remain. Rain keeps its actual preceding one-hour interval, including midnight. Explanations, full source attribution, wave definition, units, original data, timestamps and build version stay in the same immutable local snapshot detail. Data problems remain visible as concise warnings, with a count when more than two apply. No star, fishing-success score, new favorable-condition label or unsupported 'offshore' characterization is introduced.

## What does not change

Preferences, catalogs, loader.js, main.js, app-stable.js and VERIFIED_BASELINE.md are not changed. Existing snapshot details remain readable. No claim of a new device baseline is made.
