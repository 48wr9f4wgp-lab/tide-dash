## Goal

Complete a useful target-fish recommendation without waiting for a fresh species-level bulletin by using:

1. official historical Niigata monthly fishery data as the always-available regional/season prior;
2. only species-specific, evidence-backed current-condition modifiers;
3. fresh official species signals later as a confidence booster/override.

The feature is a relative target suggestion. It does not estimate an absolute recreational catch probability.

## Historical prior

Source family:
- Niigata Prefectural Fisheries and Marine Research Institute
- major fixed-net monthly reports
- same-gear five-year monthly averages

Verified months included:
- January, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November

Intentionally missing:
- February, March, December

Missing months are not interpolated.

For September:
- Japanese jack mackerel: 8.5 t
- yellowtail group: 3.5 t
- mackerels: 1.9 t
- Japanese Spanish mackerel: 0.3 t
- sardine: 0.2 t
- Japanese flounder: 0.2 t

Yellowtail group is the sum of inada + small + medium + large yellowtail rows from the same table.

## Ranking rules v1

Base:
- score = log1p(same-month five-year fixed-net tonnes)

Allowed modifiers:
- yellowtail: small Jul-Oct seasonal-movement support from a northeastern Japan Sea archival-tag study
- mackerel: small SST compatibility support only when SST is 14.72-25.72 C, transferred from a catch-based habitat study

Not used:
- generic spring/neap tide bonus
- lunar bonus
- generic dawn/dusk bonus
- wind/wave bonus without species evidence
- surface SST for flounder bottom-temperature evidence

Confidence:
- B = official multi-year regional prior is the main evidence
- A is reserved for a compatible fresh official species-level signal
- C is reserved for transferred/supporting evidence without strong regional prior
