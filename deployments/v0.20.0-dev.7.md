## Incident
Real iPhone Home Screen evidence showed 0.20.0-dev.6 failing with "Received timeout when running script." The candidate channel was immediately rolled back to the exact dev.5 manifest before preparing this build.

## Design
- Start from the exact dev.5 source that had rendered successfully on the user's iPhone.
- Do not use the dev.6 custom fieldGraph or fieldWidget DrawContext path.
- Preserve the dev.5 graph renderer, provider logic, caches, missing-data handling, snapshots and two-stage Detail.
- Increase only the large-face tide text and next-event time.
- Replace the three equal lower cards with two larger native-stack cards for wind and wave.
- Keep rain as a concise separate previous-hour line with its actual interval.
- Keep small and medium behavior unchanged.
