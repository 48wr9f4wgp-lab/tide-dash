## Fail-closed behavior

The updater/client reject:
- non-`VPWS50` payloads;
- non-official compact snapshots;
- incomplete target-area coverage;
- source report timestamps older than 120 minutes;
- source timestamps more than 15 minutes in the future;
- missing target warning areas.

Client cache:
- normal cache: 10 minutes;
- fallback: maximum 60 minutes and only while the underlying VPWS50 report still satisfies freshness validation.

A stale compact snapshot is never interpreted as “no warning”.
