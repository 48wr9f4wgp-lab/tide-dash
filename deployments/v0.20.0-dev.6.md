## Presentation changes

1. Wind and waves become two equal large cards, not a universal safety ranking. Numeric font rises from 22 to 28 points. No catch probability or safe/unsafe score is introduced.
2. Tide direction and next official high/low remain prominent. Display time, hourly tide-prediction interval, reference station/distance and weather valid time remain visible. Tomorrow is identified when applicable.
3. The full rolling tide curve and cm scale remain. Only the next two official event times are labelled. Labels are packed to avoid losing the second event, with leaders pointing to the actual time markers. Midnight retains both 00:00 and its date. Solar labels have a dedicated readable rail.
4. Rain is a separate concise line with its actual preceding-hour interval next to the amount. It is not relabelled as the next hour's forecast or an observation.
5. Solid dark background and higher-contrast supporting text replace low-contrast small annotations. Declared text/background pairs measured at least 8.61:1, including warning text. This calculation is not native accessibility certification or an outdoor sunlight test.
6. Refresh has a 44x44-point stack and Detail has a 72x44-point stack. Native tap behavior remains unverified.
7. Data warnings stay conspicuous. When needed, the graph contracts to reserve warning space rather than removing the warning. Multiple warnings retain a count and full evidence remains in Detail.
