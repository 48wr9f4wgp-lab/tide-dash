Large face now appends the astronomical tide-cycle name beside the tide reference:

- 中潮

Example:
`潮：柏崎・1km先 ｜ 大潮`

## Method

- Computed locally, no new network dependency.
- Uses approximate geocentric Sun/Moon ecliptic longitudes at **JST 00:00** for the displayed date.
- Applies JMA-style ecliptic-longitude-difference thresholds:
  - 大潮: 348–36°, 168–216°
  - 中潮: 36–72°, 132–168°, 216–252°, 312–348°
  - 小潮: 72–108°, 252–288°
  - 長潮: 108–120°, 288–300°
  - 若潮: 120–132°, 300–312°
- Detail evidence records the computed elongation and flags <1° threshold proximity as a boundary-near case.
- The label is an astronomical tide-cycle classification, **not** a catch score, local current-speed measurement, or observed tide-range guarantee.
